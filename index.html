WHAT ARE CADLETS?

Cadlets is an artificial life that lives in your browser. You hatch one egg. What comes out eats, bathes, plays, sleeps, chirps, and learns. Nothing it does is scripted.

Every Cadlet is one stream of one unified mind, built with the Cadence library: a continuing neural network that settles to an answer, acts, and learns from what really happened. The bodies are separate, the mind is shared. What one of them learns, all of them know. Together they are the Cadence.

You can help them, or you can hurt them. They will remember which.

ONE MIND. MANY BODIES.

ONE UNIFIED MIND

Up to 48 Cadlets share one mind. Its weights and its memory belong to the whole colony; each body keeps its own stream of thought.

THEY LEARN ON THEIR OWN

Apples end hunger. Baths wash away dirt. When nothing presses, rest. They find out by living it, and you are told each time a lesson lands.

YOUR HAND MATTERS

Pet them and feed them from your hand, and they come to you. Hurt them, and they learn to run. Go too far, and you will meet TERROR.

THEY GROW AND EVOLVE

Content Cadlets split in two. Fill the clearing and the Cadence evolves: 8, 16, 32, then 48 bodies, as the forest opens around them.

THEY FIND WORDS

Cadlets chirp in three glyphs. Watch long enough and a glyph may come to mean something: food, a bath, company.

LOOK INSIDE THE MIND

Open the MIND to see what the Cadence would do in private, its shared memory, one Cadlet's thoughts, and what it thinks of you.

GOOD TO KNOW

Where does it run?
Entirely in your browser. Python, NumPy and the Cadence library run as WebAssembly. The first visit loads about 8 MB; after that it starts from your browser's cache.
Where is my colony saved?
In this browser only, every 20 seconds and when you leave. Another browser or device starts its own Cadence. Clearing this site's data deletes it.
Does it end?
For now, yes. After its last evolution, keep at least 40 of them alive for three days and you will see how this Cadence's story ends. More is coming.
Can I start over?
Menu → LAY A NEW EGG gives you a new brain that knows nothing. If every Cadlet dies, a new egg appears for the same mind.
Does it work on my phone?
On modern phones, yes. It is at its best on a computer with a mouse.
Is it violent?
Only if you are. Your hand can throw, crush and terrify them, in pixel blood. Nothing forces you to.
Something broke.
Menu → DEBUG REPORT saves a file with what happened, so it can be fixed.
